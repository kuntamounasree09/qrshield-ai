Project:
QRShield AI — AI-Powered QR Code Cyber Risk Analyzer

Problem:
QR codes are widely used for payments, college registrations, job applications, advertisements, emails, posters and other activities. Users normally cannot see what is hidden inside a QR code before interacting with it. Attackers can abuse QR codes to redirect users to phishing websites, fake login pages, fake job portals, payment scams, impersonation websites, malicious downloads and suspicious redirects.

QRShield AI acts as a cybersecurity layer BEFORE the user interacts with a QR code.

Core workflow:

QR image upload
→ QR detection
→ QR decoding
→ content classification
→ cybersecurity analysis
→ deterministic risk scoring
→ AI threat analysis
→ explainable result
→ safety recommendation
→ database storage
→ scan history
